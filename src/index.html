The Eagle's Road

From a lawyer's house in Ajaccio in 1769 to a damp farm on St Helena in 1821: the battles, the turning points and the two exiles, in the order they happened.

Every stop carries its date, its place, who commanded, how many were engaged and where the account comes from. The dotted line joins the stops in order — it is not a marching route.

5 May 1821

St Helena

He died on the island in 1821, aged 51, more than 7,000 km from Paris, having spent his last years dictating the version of events he wanted remembered. In 1840 his remains were brought back to Paris, where they lie at Les Invalides.

Rumours of poisoning still circulate. Most historians accept stomach cancer, the illness that had killed his father.
