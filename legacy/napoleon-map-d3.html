The Eagle's Road

Napoleon Bonaparte's path from Corsica to St Helena, one battle and turning point at a time.

Made as a small gift for someone who knows this history far better than I do. Spot a mistake? Tell me.

Dates follow standard histories. Army sizes and casualty figures vary by source, so treat them as rough. Each stop lists the works it leans on; the standing references are David Chandler's The Campaigns of Napoleon, the Fondation Napoléon (napoleon.org) and The Napoleon Series (napoleon-series.org).
